The words move onto the keys. Alongside that: a glide decoder that learns your
hand, a held word that opens a menu, and a sweep that made every settings row
say when something else is holding it off.

### Words on the keys

**The keyboard works out which key each prediction belongs over, and draws it
there.** A flick or a tap takes the word off the key; a key carrying a word
drops its hint and reserves its lane; the words follow a stroke while it is
still being drawn; and a screen reader hears what each key is offering. A long
word shrinks rather than vanishing, and the keys stop inventing words the
ranking turned down.

### Glide typing

- **The keys move to where this hand lands on them**, and the hand model reads
  a small or large hand as a trend.
- **A stroke can be answered before it has spelled the word**, and the word
  stops changing under the finger drawing it.
- **A loop drawn on a key reads as the letter twice.**
- **Undoing a swiped word sends the stroke back for a deeper look**, and the
  keyboard remembers what you did with a stroke's reading — it learns how you
  draw each word.
- The picker arcs around the finger, best word nearest; the strip can carry the
  one word the stroke reads
  ([#84](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/84)); a pause no
  letter claims counts against the word; a swipe can answer out of your own
  words alone.
- The trail is filled as a ribbon instead of strung from beads.

### Suggestions and learning

- **Hold a word for add, delete and a word card**, with a word menu you
  configure and rank offsets you control.
- **Autocorrect learns from the typos you fix by hand.**
- A word counts once you have read it back, not before.
- A transposition is priced by the hands that made it.
- Each field's language starts from the app's habit.

### The keyboard itself

- **Keep the keyboard on screen without a text field**
  ([#58](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/58)).
- The themes tool becomes the appearance tool, and picks the emoji face too.
- The on-key bubble keeps its own height and clears the key
  ([#87](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/87)).

### Fancy text and selection

Nine more styles, two of them needing more than a letter table, and a Fancy
chip that restyles the selection in place — opening the styles rather than
guessing one.

### Updates

**The builds Play cannot reach now update themselves**: a GitHub-channel build
checks for a release, downloads it and installs it.

### Settings

- **A slider can be drawn off, the way a toggle can.**
- **Every row that depends on another switch now says so and stands down** —
  privacy, voice, clipboard, modes, selection macros, suggestions, emoji
  recents, plugins, backup encryption, data saver, the AI output rows, the app
  lock and the media list all went through this sweep.
- A setting can be found by the name another keyboard gave it.
- Rows a theme pins say so and stand down
  ([#88](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/88)).
- A row's value is measured against the row, not a cap
  ([#97](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/97)).

### Rows

Symbol row entries hold to a popup, the row stacks up to four lines, and the
keys themselves take a slot in the row order
([#83](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/83)).

### Languages

A key can carry several letters, with two boards that use it. Vietnamese Telex
stops toning words that are not syllables.

### Vocabulary

Every list is a pack, translations follow your languages, and there is a word
for everyone. The word of the day can be put away, and arrives without a jolt.
Glosses are paired, list names shown in full, quotations made readable, and a
look-up works from any related word.
