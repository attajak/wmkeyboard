Backups go to any number of places and keep your devices in sync, the keyboard
is quicker under your fingers, T9 keypads reach over 300 languages, and the voice,
translate, clipboard and sticker tools each pick up a feature people asked for.

### Backup and sync

- **Back up to any number of locations at once**: a folder, WebDAV, S3, FTP,
  SFTP, SMB, a Git repository, an IMAP mailbox, Google Drive (hidden app data
  or a folder you can see), Dropbox and OneDrive, with presets for Nextcloud,
  ownCloud and the common S3 hosts. Each location has its own sign-in, a
  connection test and its status on its card.
- **Restore straight from a location**: pick any automatic backup there, from
  this device or another.
- **Sync settings between devices** through those locations, or over
  Tailscale, and keep the toolbar or the layouts different on each device
  ([#308](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/308)).
- An offline run no longer asks you to sign in again, OneDrive stays signed in
  past 90 days, and big backups upload to Drive and OneDrive in pieces.

### Speed

- **Typing does less work per key.** A keystroke no longer lays out the whole
  keyboard window, every key draws on a layer of its own, the long-press popup
  is built before the hold fires, and suggestions run one pass at a time
  instead of one per core
  ([#313](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/313)).
- **Less memory**: layouts load when used and the big word indexes are packed
  ([#296](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/296)).
- **Accessibility › Settings app › Screen transitions** turns off the flying
  row-to-heading animation, which is what makes settings lag on a slower phone.

### Voice

- **Dictation listens for your own words**: names and jargon the keyboard has
  learned come back spelled your way
  ([#305](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/305)).
- **Dictate into the keyboard's own fields**: the AI chat, the searches, Find
  and replace, the clip editor and the rest carry a mic on their strip
  ([#353](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/353)).
- The mic stops looking open the moment it stops hearing, so no words are lost
  talking into a finished recording
  ([#315](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/315)).

### Translate, AI and look-ups

- **DeepL Translate and DeepL Write** with your own API key or a proxy
  ([#331](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/331)).
- **On-device language menus list what you downloaded first**, and keep the
  list short ([#324](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/324)).
  On Google Play, translation is on the device by default.
- **Ask the AI about a passage**: Wikipedia, Dictionary, Vocabulary and
  Grammar check hand selected text to the AI chat, with Explain simply,
  Summarise and Hard words to start from
  ([#348](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/348),
  [#352](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/352)).
- **Synonyms from the held-word menu** on the suggestion strip
  ([#321](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/321)). The
  Dictionary tool asks a chain of sources, installed vocabulary packs and
  Wiktionary among them, instead of one that was often down.
- **Search by photo** from the camera tool or the image search panel, with
  Lens, a search site, or the share sheet
  ([#349](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/349)). Web and
  image search keep their results until you start a new search
  ([#347](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/347)).
- **The text scanner reads Bengali, Arabic, Cyrillic, CJK and more** with
  Tesseract, in the full build
  ([#306](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/306)).

### Layouts and languages

- **T9 keypads for over 300 languages**, laid out the way the phone standard
  has them, Indic, Southeast Asian and tone-marked scripts included, so
  switching language keeps you on a keypad
  ([#332](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/332)). Four
  languages that downloaded a word list in the wrong script get the right one.
- **Layouts are picked from cards that preview the real keyboard** in your
  theme.
- **The 🌐 key sits in the same place on every layout**
  ([#310](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/310)), and can go
  back to the last language like Alt+Tab
  ([#311](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/311)).
- **Hold a number-row digit for the other numeral system**, 0-9 on an Arabic
  keyboard and the other way round
  ([#309](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/309)).
- **Japanese**: edit a key's flick directions, a key that doubles as 小゛゜
  while composing, and a full-width space
  ([#339](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/339),
  [#340](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/340),
  [#341](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/341)).
- **Keyman keyboards type what their rules say**, keep every layer they define,
  and fall back to a checked copy of the rules when keyman.com does not answer.
- A key map for Avro and Hindi phonetic on the language screen.
- A spacebar flick moves one language at a time, and the language name
  lingers for less time after it.
- An Arabic or Hebrew phone language no longer mirrors the keyboard
  ([#336](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/336)), and an
  English auto-shift no longer carries onto Arabic.

### Typing

- **Brackets can close themselves**
  ([#319](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/319)), and a closing
  bracket wraps a selection too
  ([#338](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/338)).
- **Hyphenated words compose whole**: "well-pai" completes to "well-paid".
- **Glide**: a loop is the one way to draw a doubled letter, while a pause or
  a wiggle marks a letter you mean, so write, wire and wrote come apart
  ([#337](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/337)). A word drawn
  in full beats a guess ([#317](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/317)),
  leaving Automatic resets what it had learned
  ([#316](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/316)), word-list
  stutters like "n-no" stay off the strip
  ([#304](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/304)), and the
  number of shapes kept per word is a setting
  ([#326](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/326)).
- **Up-flick for a capital, a 🌐 typing guard and 🌐-drag shortcuts**, all off
  by default. Dragging from shift shows the capitals and lights the key
  ([#345](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/345)).
- **Trackpad magnifier**: a bubble over the caret while the trackpad or the
  spacebar moves it
  ([#303](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/discussions/303)).
- No automatic space after the dot inside an email address, and URL fields
  keep the comma key.

### Clipboard and stickers

- **Undo after deleting a clip**
  ([#327](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/327)), and a switch
  to turn off swipe-to-delete
  ([#344](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/344)).
- Clip card options, a per-clip length limit, and rounded keyboard corners.
- **Your own stickers are offered as you type** a title, keyword or emoji,
  on a sticker row of their own
  ([#329](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/329)).

### Tools

- **The app launcher opens apps floating or in split screen**, and pairs two
  apps side by side, in ColorOS's floating window too.
- **Hide a tool from the toolbox** without turning it off.
- The symbol row: hold an entry for a menu
  ([#323](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/323)).
- An ask-first chip on the strip shows its whole question, and holding it opens
  the setting ([#312](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/312)).
- A held Trackpad tool no longer hides the toolbox while you customize it
  ([#320](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/320)).

### Themes and settings

- **Import Gboard themes and Rboard packs** from a ZIP, or browse Rboard's
  collection in the GitHub and F-Droid builds.
- **Pick the app's own language** in About
  ([#322](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/322)).
- An icon on every setting row, and an Icons in settings switch for plain rows.
- Motion across the keyboard and the settings app: panels fade in, layer
  changes ease in, and the emoji tabs switch in one smooth step.

### Automation and privacy

- **Let Tasker, adb and other apps control the keyboard**: switch layout or
  language, back up and more, behind a master switch and one switch per
  action, all off by default. Typing text is left out of the Play build.
- **Builds without the internet permission**: every network feature steps
  aside cleanly ([#292](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/292)).
- One-time camera and mic grants work, instead of sending you to Settings
  ([#351](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/351)).

### Fixes

- **Bottom padding**: no empty band above three-button navigation on
  Android 15, gesture navigation keeps its padding on ColorOS 15, and a reset
  returns it to automatic
  ([#343](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/343)).
- A floating keyboard stays put after a drag and while resizing
  ([#342](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/342)).
- Tool panels can grow the keyboard again, so the AI chat and Translate are not
  squeezed.
- Landscape: tool panels give way to the keys, and space swipes stay swipes
  ([#333](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/333)).
- No full-screen editor on tablets and unfolded foldables.
- On a TV the keyboard is a floating card, like Gboard's.
- KDE Connect says when the computer cannot do something, and an unpair
  always reaches the other device.
