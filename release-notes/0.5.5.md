A maintenance release, almost entirely about getting the F-Droid build to pass
their machines rather than only ours.

### F-Droid

- The recipe takes the exact shape F-Droid's CI accepts, formatted by the
  `fdroidserver` version they actually run.
- The recipe deletes what their scanner cannot reason about, and the tree
  survives what F-Droid does to it before building.
- **An F-Droid build downloads nothing nobody asked for.**

### Settings

- **The path strip's steps travel between screens** instead of blinking, and
  change colour rather than flashing.
