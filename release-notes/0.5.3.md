The Vocabulary tool arrives, the selection bar starts offering what to do with
what you selected, and the settings path strip becomes a row of pills.

### The Vocabulary tool

Word packs, nudges, word cards and flashcards, with a catalogue listing the
three built packs and the languages they translate into. Learn words while you
type rather than in a separate app.

### Selection

**The keyboard offers what to do with what you selected.** A bar of actions
appears when a selection exists.

### Smart chips and autocorrect

- **Long numbers come back grouped**, lakh-aware.
- **The word you typed comes back on a chip** when the fix was worth
  questioning, in a colour of your own choosing.

### Layouts and panels

- **The panels are tabs of every layout**
  ([#63](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/63)), each with a
  theme of its own, and the Numpad joins them.
- **Per-key alternates columns**
  ([#64](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/64)).
- A custom-layout picker on the tool, and persistence on panel layouts
  ([#62](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/62),
  [#60](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/60)).
- Three-set Korean and Ho Warang Citi grids.

### Handwriting

The model download gets a real progress bar and a real size, and reports what
it can see arriving. Model sizes are per language, not a flat 20 MB.

### Resize

Side handles, a bottom edge that stays put, and a still frame while you drag.

### Keys

- **Shift joins the modifier drag**, and types the capital
  ([#67](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/67)).
- Corner hints sit higher and read smaller.

### Settings

- **The path strip is a row of pills**, ending on the screen you are on, in the
  section's colour, and its last pill scrolls the screen back to the top.
- The Tools list gets a search field, its groups fold, and a closed group names
  what it holds.
- Choice sheets open whole, their options wear glyphs, and an option name that
  says nothing gets a line that does.
- Backing out of a search result lands on the same results.

### Fixed

- The word list stays expanded after you delete a word.
- The dictionaries are built from your stored settings, not the defaults.
- The glide word pill and picker get their own window
  ([#65](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/65),
  [#86](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/86)).
- The four autopilot faults from
  [#76](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/76).
- The symbols page is reachable without losing the space
  ([#34](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/34)).
