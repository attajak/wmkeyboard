The biggest release so far. Swipe typing gets a second chance at every word,
prediction learns from words further back, the settings app was rebuilt around
its own colour and motion, and the keyboard now runs on a television.

### Swipe typing

- **A swiped word can be searched again, against everything.** Pick *Search
  again* from the word menu and the stroke you drew is re-run against the whole
  dictionary instead of the trimmed swipe vocabulary. The word you pick teaches
  the decoder the stroke ([#135](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/135)).
- **Hyphenated words can be glided** — swipe straight through the hyphen
  ([#230](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/230)).
- **The three decoder tolerances are sliders now** — how far off a key a point
  may land at the start, in the middle and at the end of a stroke
  ([#222](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/222)).
- **Corrections teach the decoder however you make them.** Backspacing over a
  swiped word and retyping it used to teach nothing; now every route to a fix
  feeds the shape store, and a backup can carry your swipe style with it
  ([#213](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/213)).
- **The octopus keys answer for themselves.** A glide reads what a key actually
  carries, a tap takes the word off the key, and a flick honours what may still
  appear ([#166](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/166),
  [#209](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/209)).
- The strip says so when a language has no swipe word list
  ([#219](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/219)), the ambiguity
  picker shows what a trigger expands to, and a glided shortcut previews its
  expansion.

### Prediction and correction

- **The word three back vouches for a candidate.** Skip-grams over one and two
  skipped words feed both the correction ranking and the next-word strip
  ([#195](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/195)).
- **A missing apostrophe comes back, in every language** — not only English and
  French, and wherever the word ends
  ([#240](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/240)).
- **French keeps its space in front of `!` `?` `;` `:`**, and an elision typed
  without its apostrophe is restored — `cest` becomes `c'est`, and `l'` ends the
  word ([#215](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/215)).
- A strip pick keeps the capital of the word it replaces
  ([#212](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/212)), one shifted
  glide no longer capitalises a whole wordlist entry
  ([#154](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/154)), and a phrase
  from Android's dictionary keeps its capitals to itself.
- A dropped-letter split now needs both a low tap and a known word pair, so
  `config` stops becoming `co fig`.

### Your dictionary

- **Learn from text** takes a whole field, shows every word in it the keyboard
  does not know, and adds the ones you tick — plus the word pairs around them
  ([#174](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/174)).
- **Import from and export to Android's own personal dictionary**, through the
  system file pickers.
- The word card's spelling bar edits like a real text field: caret, selection,
  glide and scrub all work in it
  ([#204](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/204)).
- Downloading an "Everything" wordlist no longer runs out of memory
  ([#203](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/203)).

### Keys, layers and gestures

- **Any key can repeat while it is held**, on its own timing
  ([#231](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/231)).
- **A held delete key can clear whole words, and `⌦` swipes**
  ([#216](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/216),
  [#226](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/226)).
- **A kept layer survives the keyboard closing** — and process death with it
  ([#227](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/227)).
- **Select all is a toolbar tool of its own**
  ([#228](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/228)).
- Shift capitalises the long-press popup and the corner hint
  ([#211](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/211)); the drag off
  `?123` or `ABC` draws its band again, from a panel too
  ([#108](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/108)); the press and
  hold delay reaches down to 100 ms.
- **å, ä and ö get keys of their own on the Nordic boards** instead of hiding
  under a long press.
- Enter breaks the line in a box that asked for one
  ([#214](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/214)), does not leave
  the word it sent being composed
  ([#236](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/236)), and a shift
  trades the enter action for the line break.
- Typing shows up as you type in a terminal
  ([#220](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/220)), and the
  keyboard's own text boxes type like real fields
  ([#161](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/161)).
- The spacebar language preview slides between languages and stays one row with
  long labels.

### Android TV

The keyboard runs on a television. A remote drives it, the D-pad ring holds a
key open for its alternates and follows a floating board, and every layout is
redrawn as an even ten-column grid.

### Themes and appearance

- **A single key can carry its own image, burst and label** — per-key hint,
  texture, weight and size, out of a glyph bank
  ([#107](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/107)).
- **Pick an icon for any key**, from every icon the app draws
  ([#223](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/223)).
- Toolbar top and bottom padding, and where a key's hint sits, are yours to set.
- A theme's key sound never silences the one you picked.

### Emoji

The panel's categories go in the order you put them, and the ones you do not
want are gone ([#183](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/183)).

### The settings app

- **It wears its own colours and answers a press**, uses a tablet's full width,
  and a screen that is still opening looks like it is.
- **A card, a tile or a row grows into the page it opens**, and search results
  rise in and then travel as you type.
- **Every capped list gets a scroll rail**, which can carry an index
  ([#202](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/202)).
- Deleting a row can be taken back. A moved toolbox tool lands where it was
  dropped. Row order names the suggestions and tools rows apart.
- "Only offer new words" and "Numbers from the letter keys" show on Android 10
  and lower again, and search opens instead of crashing the app.

### Faster

- **The key preview bubble draws in the keyboard's own window**, which takes a
  window handoff out of every keypress.
- A slider writes once, when the finger lifts, instead of on every frame of the
  drag.
- The on-demand tools row opens without resizing the window
  ([#217](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/217)), and a settings
  group bigger than the entrance budget waits its turn.

### Voice

Whisper Tiny (English) transcribes instead of returning nothing.

### On the web

The [docs site](https://wmkeyboard.pages.dev) gained an **addon store** at
`/addons` with builders for every addon type, a Lua sandbox, a font workshop and
the app's plugin editor running in the browser — plus a link builder and an
`/open/` doorway that opens the screen it names in the app.
