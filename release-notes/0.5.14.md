Flick keys with eight directions, handwriting over the whole screen and in
Japanese, Chinese and Korean, Khipro glide typing, and a clipboard and
suggestion strip rebuilt around the requests in their threads. The AI can
search the web, dictation can be tidied by AI and told which language to
listen for, and the keyboard holds far less memory when it sits idle.

### Typing and suggestions

- **Eight-direction flick keys**, with their arms drawn on the key face
  ([#410](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/410)); flicks can run actions and have a Shift form of their own
  ([#549](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/549), [#550](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/550)).
- **The keyboard's word lists as Android's spell checker**
  ([#511](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/511)).
- **The strip can scroll through every word found**, keep its suggestions in
  place, choose how many emoji follow, show the selected word and colour
  suggestions with the theme's accent ([#516](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/516), [#509](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/509),
  [#513](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/513), [#510](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/510), [#413](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/413)). The button beside it can be
  any tool ([#462](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/462)).
- **Remember numbers and email addresses you type**
  ([#431](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/431), [#475](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/475)).
- **Correct the word a punctuation mark ends**, as an option
  ([#562](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/562)); capitals come from the word list for English and German
  ([#481](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/481), [#517](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/517)), and German gehts becomes geht's
  ([#518](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/518)).
- Word-pair packs for 48 more languages, so the next word is guessed in them
  too.
- Spacebar and cursor: drag the cursor past the spacebar's edge, use the whole
  keyboard as a touchpad, move up and down directly in one-line fields
  ([#505](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/505)); a swipe can open Android's keyboard list
  ([#477](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/477)), and the cursor's vibration can be turned off
  ([#466](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/466)).
- Long-press alternates: a readable highlight, the first alternate over the
  key, steps one at a time, and the classic popup in Accessibility
  ([#532](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/532)); the order of a letter's popup groups is yours to set
  ([#385](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/385)).
- A deleted word stays off the strip after a glide ([#277](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/277)), a
  contraction can be glided without its apostrophe ([#451](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/451)), and a
  shift after the punctuation space capitalizes ([#542](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/542)).

### Layouts and languages

- **Use a layout from another language** ([#464](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/464)), and turn on a
  layout of your own without hunting for the switch.
- **Khipro glide typing**, spelling every word on the phone
  ([#541](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/541)), with English words typed as English ([#487](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/487)) and
  the danda building ॥ and ৺ ([#488](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/488), [#489](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/489)). Avro's
  candidate list is desktop Avro's own, and its dictionary is a download.
- **Hindi native Devanagari** ([#498](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/498)), **Urdu phonetic**
  ([#496](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/496)), Persian laid out like Gboard ([#506](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/506)) and
  phonetic layouts for eleven more languages; a half-typed word carries onto
  a phonetic layout ([#522](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/522)).
- **Klingon** with capitals, pIqaD, numbers and glide; Klingon and Lojban stay on
  once added ([#566](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/566)).
- **Vietnamese Telex and VNI catch up with bamboo** ([#467](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/467)).
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean: pick candidates with space, the arrows and
  Enter ([#419](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/419)), convert in the keyboard's own text fields
  ([#527](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/527)), reach pairings from pinyin initials ([#528](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/528)),
  your own Double Pinyin scheme ([#502](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/502)), and katakana fixes
  ([#514](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/514), [#515](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/515), [#526](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/526), [#529](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/529)).
- **Import Samsung Keyboard layouts** shared from Keys Cafe (.kcf).
- A 1234 key on the symbol pages and a numpad in a ?123 popup
  ([#423](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/423)); the ?123 page leads with your currency; edge keys of a
  short row take the space beside them ([#530](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/530)); small tablets get
  the arrow keys ([#553](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/553)).

### Clipboard, snippets and panels

- **The clipboard can sit over the live keys**, shows recent copies as chips
  on the strip, searches by regex, marks found parts on each clip and can type
  a clip out one character at a time ([#414](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/414), [#472](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/472),
  [#418](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/418)).
- Snippets: search, a column count, triggers in a browser's address bar and
  an opt-in for password fields ([#471](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/471), [#555](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/555),
  [#554](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/554), [#552](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/552)).
- Emoji: a scrolling tab strip, one long list and a height slider for the
  emoji, GIF and sticker panels ([#539](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/539), [#540](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/540),
  [#537](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/537), [#538](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/538)).

### Voice, handwriting, AI and tools

- **Handwriting over the whole screen** ([#386](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/386)) and in Japanese,
  Chinese and Korean ([#557](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/557)).
- **Tidy dictation with AI** ([#499](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/499)), **pick the language dictation
  listens for** ([#416](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/416)), end a Whisper clip on a pause
  ([#500](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/500)) and pause media while you talk ([#485](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/485)). English
  Whisper models no longer drop short phrases ([#440](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/440)).
- **The AI can search the web and read a page**, and shows which it used
  ([#470](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/470)); a password field's text never leaves the phone.
- An online text-from-photo engine ([#469](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/469)), a paste button in
  Translate ([#474](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/474)), and a switch to stop update checks
  ([#507](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/507)).
- **Per-key sounds and a sound pack per layout** ([#520](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/520)).

### Themes, icons and settings

- Every icon drawn from Material Symbols, with other looks per icon slot and
  slots for the arrow row; themes gain a key shadow colour, toolbar fill,
  other-suggestion colour and popup highlight, and imports carry key glyphs
  ([#504](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/504)).
- A keyboard test button on every settings screen ([#412](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/412)), and the
  English font you pick wins over layout and theme fonts ([#561](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/561)).

### Memory and fixes

- **Hotfix (versionCode 31): no more gap under the space bar.** The first
  0.5.14 build left a gap about a row high below the keyboard on many phones,
  and made it flicker on some; the keyboard's window now reaches the bottom
  edge on every Android version and keeps clear of the navigation bar by the
  bar's own height ([#569](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/569)).
  The word-list spell checker also lists every language again in full builds
  ([#511](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/511)).
- **Much less memory held**: heavy engines are freed when the keyboard goes
  idle, pictures are sampled instead of decoded whole, and big caches are
  bounded ([#476](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/476)).
- Codes typed one digit per box land right ([#508](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/508)); backspace at the
  start of a field reaches the app ([#494](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/494)); the strip and clipboard
  return after the lock screen ([#492](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/492)); the navigation bar inset is
  read from the window ([#468](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/468)).
- A field opens in the language another app remembered ([#484](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/484)),
  copies after leaving a private field are kept ([#551](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/551)), and the
  selection bar unblocks ([#525](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/525)).
