Alternates on every key, autocorrect that waits for the text to settle, and the
Keyman catalogue gets a page of its own.

### Layouts

- **Alternates on every key**, actions inside the popup, and popups that wrap
  instead of running off the edge.
- Each layer can set its own label size.
- A role belongs to the text a key types, not to the key.

### Languages

- **The Keyman catalogue moves to a More layouts page** instead of crowding the
  language list.

### Typing

- **Autocorrect judges a correction once the text settles**, rather than at the
  moment of the keystroke.
- A new word has to earn its place in the personal dictionary.
- Enter types the line break rather than pressing the key, where the field
  asked for one.

### Fixed

- Shift lock selects on every caret path, and lets go afterwards.
- A glide keeps the space when a line break follows the caret.
- Espanso patterns compile the way Android's regex engine actually behaves.
- The panel layout editor stops nesting two scrollers.

### Settings

- Voice typing settings move to their own Features screen.
