Emoji, GIFs and stickers share one panel, the clipboard grows taller and reads
text off images, typing statistics arrive with levels and a heatmap, and new
layouts reach Korean, Bengali, Arabic, Marathi and Hebrew. Voice, AI and
Translate can fall back to the models on the phone when the connection is gone.

### Panels, clipboard and stickers

- **Switch between the emoji, GIF and sticker panels** from a row in any of
  them ([#366](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/366)).
- **Import WhatsApp sticker packs**
  ([#368](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/368)), and hold a
  suggested sticker to see it bigger
  ([#404](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/404)).
- **The clipboard reads text off images, opens and views clips**, with tabs,
  clear and outlines ([#371](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/371)).
  The panel can be dragged taller, shows a clip's full text, takes optional
  buttons on each card and keeps as many clips as you like
  ([#414](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/414)).
- **Deleting a clip clears it from the system clipboard too**
  ([#442](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/442)), and in
  incognito the keyboard's own copies stay off it
  ([#392](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/392)).

### Typing

- **Hold shift with one finger and type capitals with another**
  ([#367](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/367)).
- **An arrow keys row** under the keyboard
  ([#369](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/369)).
- **Long spacebar cursor swipes speed up**
  ([#385](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/385)), and ?123
  can go back to the letters after a space
  ([#359](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/359)).
- **Leave the word before Enter as typed**: a switch so a search sent with
  Enter is not corrected on the way
  ([#457](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/457)).
- **Backspace after a glide can delete one letter** instead of the whole word
  ([#455](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/455)).
- **Swipe in from the keyboard's edge to go back**, for phones that keep the
  system's back gesture off the keyboard
  ([#437](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/437)).
- Glide: crossing the spacebar only splits a word once the stroke reaches the
  bar's middle ([#428](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/428)).
- Autocorrect: backspace undoes a repaired apostrophe or capital I
  ([#402](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/402)), "ill" offers
  I'll ([#384](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/384)), German
  "im" stays "im" ([#425](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/425)),
  and English verb forms the word list lacks are left alone
  ([#395](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/395)).
- **Dutch "wel", "nog" and "ging" are suggested again**: another language's
  offensive-word list no longer hides them
  ([#465](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/465)).
- Tap a slider's value to type it exactly
  ([#415](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/415)), and the
  spacebar's language echo can be tuned or turned off
  ([#376](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/376)).

### Layouts and languages

- **Korean 천지인 (Cheonjiin) keypad**, and Hangul now builds syllables in the
  keyboard's own search boxes too
  ([#454](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/454)).
- **Bengali Khipro layout**
  ([#400](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/400)) and ANSI
  (Bijoy) output; Avro conjuncts and ঙ্গ follow desktop Avro.
- **Arabic with every letter on a key** and no shift key
  ([#427](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/427)), Marathi
  shifted letters on hold, Hebrew SI-1452, QWERTZ for English
  ([#422](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/422)), and layouts
  to match HeliBoard, FlorisBoard and FUTO.
- **Jianpin** (abbreviated pinyin)
  ([#405](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/405)).
- Persian suggests words with their half-space and puts the harakat on shift
  ([#406](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/406),
  [#407](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/407),
  [#393](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/393)); Polish puts
  diacritics first on hold
  ([#362](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/362)); every grid
  has the question mark one hold away
  ([#408](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/408)); a split
  keyboard can keep the spacebar whole
  ([#399](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/399)).
- The number pad's space key shows ␣
  ([#459](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/459)), and the
  layout editor draws the bottom row the keyboard draws
  ([#420](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/420)).

### Voice, Translate, AI and search

- **Use on-device models offline**: with no connection, or a server that does
  not answer, voice typing, AI actions and Translate use the model on the
  phone ([#452](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/452)).
- **Your own translation server**, OpenAI-compatible
  ([#435](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/435)), and Translate
  opens on the selection with a chip beside AI
  ([#434](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/434)).
- **Brave Answers as an AI provider**
  ([#438](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/438)) and **Tavily**
  for web and image search
  ([#439](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/439)).
- A transcription server per language, with its own endpoint path
  ([#388](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/388),
  [#389](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/389)).

### Statistics, themes and settings

- **Typing statistics with levels, achievements, a tap heatmap** and a
  Statistics tool ([#390](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/390));
  synced statistics add up per device
  ([#447](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/447)).
- Image sizes and templates for theme designers
  ([#397](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/397)).
- Install language packs and offline GIF packs from a file.
