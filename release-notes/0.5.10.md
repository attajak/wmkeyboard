Hindi you can type phonetically, English words that stay English on Avro and
Hindi, and an importer that reads what FlorisBoard, HeliBoard, LeanType, FUTO
and AOSP keyboards leave behind. The app now speaks 48 languages outside Play,
and the keyboard fits the screen in landscape.

### Installed from F-Droid? Reinstall once

From this version, F-Droid's copy is signed with the same key as the APKs
below: F-Droid still builds it from source, then publishes it only if it is
identical to `wmkeyboard-0.5.10-vc22-fdroid.apk` here. Installs from F-Droid of
0.5.9 or older carry F-Droid's own key, and Android won't update across keys,
so F-Droid can't move you to 0.5.10 by itself. Export a backup
(**Backup & restore › Export Backup**), uninstall, install WM Keyboard from
F-Droid again, and import the backup (**Backup & restore › Import Backup**).
Every update after that is ordinary, and you can move between the F-Droid and
GitHub builds without uninstalling.

### Hindi phonetic

- **Latin keys in, Devanagari out.** `kaise ho` commits कैसे हो, the way Avro
  works for Bengali. It is the second layout in Hindi's list, so enabling Hindi
  still gives InScript
  ([#239](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/239)).
- **It guesses where Hinglish spelling says nothing**: whether `rn` in karna is
  two syllables and `st` in namaste one conjunct, long vowels at a word's end,
  an anusvara before its stop. The other readings wait on the strip.
- **With the Hindi word list downloaded it knows the words**, forgiving dropped
  aspiration, dental against retroflex and every missing inner "a". A strip chip
  points at the download until there is one. On 183 everyday Hinglish words,
  rules alone get 58% right and rules with the word list 97%.
- Glide typing works on it, against a spelling map and the Hinglish list.
- **To Hindi and To Hinglish** join the selection actions, the typing test
  ships a Hindi word pool and quotations, and Read aloud picks a Hindi voice for
  Devanagari.

### English words on Avro and Hindi phonetic

- **One English word in a Bangla message no longer means switching layouts
  twice.** Add English under the language's *Also suggest from*, and the strip
  always carries what you typed in Latin letters.
- **"Type English words as English"** (off by default, in the language's new
  *English words* group) lets the space bar commit English when a word reads as
  English. The word before decides a close call: `am` after `i` stays English,
  and `ok to bolo` keeps its তো.
- Guessed wrong? Backspace straight after it, tap the undo chip, or pick the
  other script's chip. Two flips settle that spelling for good.
- An **English words** toolbar tool switches it, and redraws the word you are
  in the middle of.

### Import from other keyboards

- **FlorisBoard themes arrive as the theme you picked**, not an approximation:
  pressed and caps-lock states, multi-key rules, Material You colours, Snygg v1
  themes, round enter keys, key shadows and the typeface a theme ships with.
  The import dialog stops reporting losses that aren't
  ([#266](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/266)).
- **HeliBoard and LeanType colour themes**, pasted or from a file.
- **FUTO Keyboard layouts.** 166 of the 167 in FUTO's own repository convert.
- **AOSP word lists**, both `.combined` source files and compiled `.dict`
  files. A `.combined` list used to import as junk.
- **HeliBoard layouts keep their special keys**: delete, currency, case
  selectors and popup markers stop turning into keys that type their names.
- **A FlorisBoard Chinese language pack fills the Cangjie and stroke tables**
  with no download, with its character ranking intact.

### Opening and sharing files

- **Share a link into the app and it imports what is behind it**: a file on
  GitHub, GitLab or another forge, a release, a gist, a repository, or an
  Actions artifact. Also from a paste field on the Add-ons screen.
- **The import dialog shows what you are importing**: a theme or layout as a
  miniature, a sticker pack's stickers, a sound pack you can play, the first
  entries of a snippet or vocabulary pack.
- **Tapping one of our files in Downloads reaches the app**, even when the
  file manager hands over a link with no file name in it. Sound packs are
  recognised, and files can be shared in from a chat app.
- **A YAML file opens as what it is**: a FUTO layout or an Espanso match file.
  Anything else readable opens in the code editor instead of crashing.

### Typing and suggestions

- **The word the cursor is in leads the strip**, so it can be added, blocked
  or opened from its menu without going to settings
  ([#263](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/263)).
- **A strip pick replaces the whole word again**, instead of splicing into the
  wrong place in apps that restyle as you type
  ([#267](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/267)).
- **Typos a big word list counts as words are corrected.** `teh` and `thw` are
  in the Medium English list; they get fixed anyway
  ([#244](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/244)).
- The missing apostrophe comes back for every language in the mix, not only
  the one in front
  ([#240](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/240)).
- **Chinese types characters.** Pinyin converts in search boxes and other
  fields that turn suggestions off, and the strip says which pack to download
  when none is installed
  ([#260](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/260)).
- **Saved logins land in the strip** as chips in the keyboard's colours,
  instead of a password manager's popup over the keys. Turning the setting off
  gives the manager's dropdown back
  ([#250](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/250)).
- The keyboard opens in the first language of your list, not English
  ([#249](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/249)).

### Glide and gestures

- **The marks that double a letter are settings**: how long a pause, how round
  a circle and how wide a rub, next to the error radii in a new *Advanced
  tuning* fold ([#270](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/270)).
- **The 's swipe adds contractions too**: lift on `t`, `d`, `m`, `l`, `r` or
  `v` for 't, 'd, 'm, 'll, 're, 've. It draws a band, and a slow swipe no longer
  opens the key's popup ([#243](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/243)).
- A secondary language no longer switches glide off
  ([#272](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/272)).
- The *Search all words* offer is a search icon, not half the strip
  ([#264](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/264)).
- Neither a backspace swipe nor a held backspace types a glided word anymore
  ([#243](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/243),
  [#274](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/274)), and the
  possessive swipe's key never starts a glide
  ([#169](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/169)).

### Keys and layouts

- **The number row and the symbols fill row are editable** in the layout
  editor, where before they were fixed
  ([#273](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/273)).
- **A delete key on a panel is a real delete key**: it swipes, clears words
  when held, and there is a forward delete for the text-editing pad
  ([#226](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/226)).
- **Holding backspace in a terminal keeps deleting**
  ([#268](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/268)).
- **Persian matches the standard Iranian arrangement**, with a half-space key.
- The bottom row reaches both edges on boards eleven or twelve keys wide,
  Arabic and Cyrillic among 257 layouts.

### Landscape

**The keyboard fits the screen sideways.** The shift row and space bar were cut
off; now the board takes about half the screen, sized off Gboard's, with a
smaller toolbar and no number row unless you asked for one
([#251](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/251)).

### Selection

- **Remove trackers** strips `utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid` and their kin from a
  selected link and leaves the rest of it alone.
- **Open wears the icon of the app a link lands in.**

### Themes

- **Dracula gets Alucard**, its light look.
- **Themes can set key, popup, panel and toolbar shadows**, the popup's

### Emoji

- A Banglish emoji keyword pack: search in the Latin spellings people chat in.

### Languages and dictionaries

- **One Dictionaries section per language**, with a row each for the word

- **Change a downloaded word list's size in place**, without deleting it first.

### The app in your language

**The settings app now ships in 48 languages besides English** on GitHub and
F-Droid, not only from Play. The **English** builds above leave them out and are
about 41 MB smaller; either installs over the other.

### Crash reports

**A crash pasted into an issue comes back retraced.** A bot reads the
`version:` line, fetches the matching mapping from the release, and comments
the readable trace. Holding the crash text under **About › Diagnostics** copies
it with that line on top. The Play build's mapping is attached below too.
