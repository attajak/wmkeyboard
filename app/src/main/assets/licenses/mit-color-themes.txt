Colour palettes used by the built-in keyboard themes
====================================================

The built-in themes named below reproduce the colour palettes of the
following projects. Each is distributed under the MIT License, reproduced
once at the end of this file; the copyright notices are listed here.

  Dracula  ("Dracula" and "Alucard" themes)
  Copyright (c) 2023 Dracula Theme
  https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme

  Nord  ("Nord" theme)
  Copyright (c) 2016-present Sven Greb <development@svengreb.de>
  https://github.com/nordtheme/nord

  Solarized  ("Solarized Dark" and "Solarized Light" themes)
  Copyright (c) 2011 Ethan Schoonover
  https://github.com/altercation/solarized

  Catppuccin  ("Catppuccin Mocha" and "Catppuccin Latte" themes)
  Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin
  https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin

  Tokyo Night  ("Tokyo Night" theme)
  Copyright (c) enkia
  https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme

The project names are used only to identify which palette a theme
