Eight-direction flick keys. Handwriting over the whole screen and in Japanese, Chinese and Korean. Khipro glide typing, Hindi Devanagari, Urdu phonetic, Klingon. The AI can search the web; dictation can be tidied by AI and told its language. The clipboard sits over the keys, the strip scrolls every word, and the keyboard's lists work as Android's spell checker. Far less memory when idle.
