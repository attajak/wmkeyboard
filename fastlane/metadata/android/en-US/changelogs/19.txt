The selection bar gains thirty actions, Find and replace, and an Undo. Convert whole texts between Banglish and Bangla. Pick an icon for any key. Swipe down on a key to type its corner hint, or from a punctuation key to S for 's. A word typed without its accents is corrected. The word two back helps pick the next one. Panels take Shift and Fn. Hold the mic to choose a voice mode. A mode can be switched off, or bound to a field's shape. The theme editor previews on the keyboard itself.
